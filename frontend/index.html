How it works

From raw contract to recovery
in four automated steps

No manual auditing. No spreadsheets. KUBO runs a 4-agent pipeline that cross-references your contract, usage data, and invoices in one pass.

STEP 01
Parse Contract
The Contract Analyst reads your PDF agreement and extracts pricing terms, overage rates, discount scope, and billing rules.
STEP 02
Validate Usage
The Usage Validator ingests your raw API or metered usage logs, deduplicates retries, and calculates true consumption vs. contract limits.
STEP 03
Audit Billing
The Billing Auditor compares each invoice line item against contract terms — catching missing overages, wrong rates, and misapplied discounts.
STEP 04
Orchestrate Recovery
The Orchestrator aggregates all findings, ranks recovery actions by expected utility, and generates a corrective invoice and email draft — ready to send.
The agent pipeline

Four specialized agents.
One unified answer.

Each agent is an expert in its domain. Together they form a Bayesian evidence chain that's more reliable than any single model call.

CONTRACT ANALYST
Reads your contract so you don't have to
Parses the executed PDF to extract base fees, usage caps, overage rate schedules, discount scope clauses, and payment terms. Flags ambiguous language that could produce conflicting billing interpretations.
PDF PARSING CLAUSE EXTRACTION PRICING MODEL
USAGE VALIDATOR
Finds the units you were never billed for
Ingests raw API logs or metered usage data, deduplicates retry calls, and aggregates consumption by billing period. Computes true overage units using your contract's unit multiplier.
LOG ANALYSIS DEDUPLICATION OVERAGE CALC
BILLING AUDITOR
Spots every dollar difference on your invoice
Cross-references each invoice line item against your contract's pricing rules. Catches missing overage lines, wrong rate application, and discount scope errors — and quantifies the dollar impact of each.
LINE ITEM AUDIT DISCOUNT CHECK RATE VALIDATION
ORCHESTRATOR
Ranks your recovery options by expected value
Aggregates all three agent outputs using Bayesian confidence weighting. Scores each recovery action by Expected Utility, then generates a corrective invoice payload and a ready-to-send email draft.

What KUBO catches

The three most common
sources of revenue leakage

Most enterprise billing errors fall into predictable patterns. KUBO is trained to detect all of them automatically.

📋
Missed Overages
Usage exceeded contract limits but no overage line item appeared on the invoice. Common when billing systems don't read usage logs in real time.
UNBILLED UNITS
⚠️
Misapplied Discounts
Discounts intended for base charges only were incorrectly applied to overage charges, reducing revenue beyond what the contract permits.
DISCOUNT SCOPE ERROR
💰
Wrong Rate Application
The unit price used in the invoice doesn't match the contracted rate — often a stale rate from a previous billing period or pricing tier.
