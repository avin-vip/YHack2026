**End on outcome, not technology.** "The money was always yours" reframes the entire product. ARIA isn't an AI tool. It's a recovery system. That's a different conversation — one CFOs and finance stakeholders immediately understand.

**The pause before "Questions?" signals confidence.** Nervous pitchers rush to the end. A one-second hold after your last line says: *I'm done, I'm certain, I'm not afraid of what you're about to ask me.*

## The Problem: Revenue Leakage Is Silent and Massive

Revenue leakage is money you already earned — contractually owed to you — that never makes it onto an invoice. It's not fraud. It's not churn. It's billing system misconfiguration, human error, and contract complexity compounding month after month.

**How big is the problem?**

- **EY estimates** 1–5% of enterprise revenue is lost to billing errors annually
- **MGI Research** found that 42% of SaaS companies have identified revenue leakage but lack systematic tools to fix it
- **Cledara's 2024 report**: the average company overpays or underbills on 12% of its software contracts

**Put a dollar sign on it:**

| ARR | Leakage at 3% | Leakage at 5% |
|-----|---------------|---------------|
| $10M | $300K/year | $500K/year |
| $50M | $1.5M/year | $2.5M/year |
| $200M | $6M/year | $10M/year |

A $50M ARR company is losing **$1.5M to $2.5M per year** — not from losing customers, but from invoices that don't match contracts. That money is sitting on the table. The contract says they owe it. The invoice just never asked for it.

**Why hasn't this been solved?**

Because checking a single contract requires reading the pricing terms, pulling the usage data, comparing it against the invoice line by line, verifying discount scope, and confirming the math. That takes a trained analyst 2–4 hours per contract.

350 contracts × 3 hours = **1,050 hours per month** of manual audit work. That's 6+ full-time employees just to check invoices. Nobody staffs for that.

## How ARIA Detects Leakage — The 4-Agent Pipeline

This is not a single LLM call. This is not "paste your invoice into ChatGPT." ARIA runs 4 specialized agents, each with a distinct job, and they cross-validate each other's work.

### Agent 1: Contract Analyst
**Reads the contract. Extracts the rules.**
- Parses pricing structure: base fee, overage rates, usage limits
- Identifies discount scope (base only? all charges? capped?)
- Flags ambiguous clauses that create leakage risk
- Output: "Base fee $85K/mo, overage at $0.05/unit above 10K, discount applies to base charges only per §12.3"

### Agent 2: Usage Validator
**Checks actual usage against contract limits.**
- Ingests raw usage logs (API calls, seats, storage — whatever the unit is)
- Deduplicates retries, aggregates by billing period
- Compares against contract limits
- Output: "10,840 units consumed, 10,000 limit, 840 units overage = $42,000 gross exposure"

### Agent 3: Billing Auditor
**Audits the invoice against the contract.**
- Verifies every line item exists that should exist
- Checks discount was applied to the correct scope
- Catches missing overage charges, wrong rates, misapplied credits
- Output: "Overage line item missing. Discount applied to all charges — contract says base only. Underbilled by $21,250."

### Agent 4: Orchestrator
**Aggregates all findings. Calculates the number that matters.**
- Cross-references all 3 agents' evidence
- Computes net leakage with Bayesian confidence intervals
- Ranks recovery actions by (financial impact × confidence score)
- Generates a ready-to-send recovery email and corrective invoice
- Output: "Net leakage: $21,250 (95% CI: $18.4K–$24.1K). Top action: Issue corrective invoice, 92% confidence."

**Why 4 agents, not 1?**

Because a single model hallucinates. Four agents with independent evidence sources cross-validate. If the Contract Analyst says "overage rate is $0.05" and the Billing Auditor says "no overage line item exists" and the Usage Validator says "840 units over limit" — that's 3 independent signals confirming the same discrepancy. The confidence is real, not a guess.

**"How accurate is it?"**
92% detection rate on our validated dataset. Bayesian confidence intervals on every finding — we tell you not just "$21,250" but "$18.4K to $24.1K with 95% probability." If confidence is below 80%, we flag it for human review instead of auto-generating a recovery action.

**"How is this different from Stripe/billing system reconciliation?"**
